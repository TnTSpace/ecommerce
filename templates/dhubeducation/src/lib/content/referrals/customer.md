## Benefit

Enjoy exclusive rewards and dedicated academic support when you refer students and institutions to DHUB Education.

## Details

The **Customer Referral Program** at DHUB Education is designed to empower our current and past students with valuable opportunities to grow, give back, and earn. As a Customer Referral, you become part of a trusted network that promotes the transformative power of education while being rewarded for your influence.

By joining the program, you gain:

* **Referral Rewards**: Earn commissions or exclusive benefits every time someone you refer enrolls in a DHUB Education course or program.
* **Tailored Academic Support**: Receive one-on-one guidance, learning tools, and resources that help you navigate your academic and career journey successfully.
* **Community Recognition**: Be spotlighted as an ambassador for quality education and help expand access to learning in your community.

This program is open exclusively to **current and former students** of DHUB Education who are passionate about sharing the value they’ve received. Whether you're still enrolled or an alumnus, your experience makes you a powerful advocate—and now, you can turn that advocacy into tangible rewards.

To get started, simply agree to our referral program’s terms and conditions and begin sharing your unique referral link.
