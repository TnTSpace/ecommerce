## Benefit
Access supplementary resources, contribute to educational content, and engage in professional development opportunities.

## Details
The Teacher Referral program is tailored for educators who are passionate about enhancing educational experiences. Referrals in this category gain access to supplementary teaching materials and resources, along with opportunities to share their expertise and contribute to educational content. Additionally, Teacher Referrals benefit from networking and professional development opportunities within the DHUB Education community. To qualify, individuals must be currently employed as an educator or have relevant teaching experience, and they should be committed to advancing educational excellence.
